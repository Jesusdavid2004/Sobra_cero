# User guide

What each person can do, step by step. Screens are available in English and Spanish and in light,
dark or system theme (buttons in the top bar).

## Customers

1. **Browse** the offers in *Vitrine*, or open **Map** to see affiliated shops (solid discs) and
   businesses found in public sources (dashed rings). Click a marker or a list item for its card:
   affiliation, address, hours, source and when it was last seen.
2. **Find a pickup** that fits your day: write what you need ("I have 20 dollars, 2 portions of
   bakery") and press *Fill the form with AI*, or fill the form yourself. Check the values, press *Use
   my location* and *Find lots I can collect*. You see lots you can reach before they close, and the
   reason each other lot is left out.
3. **Reserve** a lot. You get a reservation reference, a code and a **pickup QR**. Show the QR at the
   shop; it works once.
4. **Cancel** a reservation from the lot page if your plans change.

## Shop owners

1. Create your **shop** with its real latitude and longitude, so it appears in the right place on the
   map and in nearby searches.
2. Add **products** and **lots** (quantity, price, discount, expiry, optional pickup deadline).
3. Read the **daily diagnosis** on *My shop*: alerts, numbers, what it cannot know, and a short AI
   summary. If the AI is unavailable the numbers are still there.
4. Open a lot's **rescue plan**: see the reasons, ask for a plain-words explanation, compare scenarios
   (hypothetical, not predictions), then approve or reject. Nothing changes without your approval.
5. If the plan suggests it, **offer part of a lot as a donation**: find compatible receivers (and why
   others were left out), draft the message with AI, edit it and send. The receiver accepts; you
   confirm the delivery. Only delivered units count as donated.
6. **Confirm pickups** with the reservation reference and code, or scan the customer's QR.
7. **Record waste** for units you could not sell or donate. It is what you declare; nothing is
   estimated.

## Receiving organizations

1. Open **Receive donations** and create your profile: food you accept, the most portions you can take,
   whether you can keep cold food, and when you are open.
2. Accept or decline each offer. Accepting commits the units once.
3. SobraCero does not verify organizations or certify food safety; agree handling directly with the
   shop.

## Administrators: the Scanner

Open **Scanner** (administrators only).

1. **Register a source** (HTTPS address on an allowed domain). It starts disabled and unreviewed.
2. **Review its terms**: read the terms of use and `robots.txt` and record what you checked. Without
   this the source cannot be enabled or run.
3. **Enable** it, optionally set a **schedule**, and press **Run now**.
4. Check **Runs** (status, counters, why a run stopped, rejected records), **Products and prices**
   (freshness, price history) and **Discovered businesses** (verify or reject). Discovered businesses
   are never affiliated automatically.
5. Out-of-date prices are marked and must not be read as current.

## Things the platform does not claim

- It does not certify that food is safe, and a QR or a receipt is not a tax document.
- A promotion on a public catalog is not a surplus, and a price is not stock.
- Distances are straight-line estimates and times are approximate.
- Receivers and businesses found in public sources are not verified partners.

## What is missing for the new goal

1. **No geography beyond a bounding-box filter.** Shops have latitude and longitude, but there is
   no map, no directory of businesses that are not customers, no geocoding.
2. **No external data pipeline.** Nothing discovers businesses or reads public catalogs; there is
   no place to record where a datum came from, when, or whether it may be used.
3. **Inventory is a quantity on a lot.** Stock changes are correct and atomic but are not recorded
   as a ledger, so "what happened to these units" can only be reconstructed from reservations,
   donations and the plan timeline. Waste is not recorded at all.
4. **No shop-level diagnosis.** Plans are per lot; there is no daily summary that explains the
   whole shop's situation.
5. **No admin view of data collection**, because there is no data collection.

## Decisions

- **Maps:** Leaflet with marker clustering. Rationale: no WebGL requirement, small, mature, fits a
  directory of a few thousand points. MapLibre would add vector tiles we do not host.
- **PostGIS: not adopted yet.** The queries needed are bounding-box and nearest-first over at most
  thousands of rows; indexed latitude and longitude plus haversine in code are enough, and the
  test database is SQLite. Revisit above roughly 50,000 places or when polygon queries appear.
- **Scraping:** Scrapy, in its own package, one spider per source, polite settings, and a policy
  layer that refuses to enable a source until a person records that its terms were reviewed.
- **External data never becomes marketplace stock.** External businesses and products live in
  their own tables; an offer exists only when an affiliated shop registers a lot.

## Order of work

P0: this audit; geography domain and directory; Scrapy base with two real connectors;
Scanner admin API and screen; map screen; ledger and waste; shop diagnosis.
P1: price history and promotion analysis; more connectors once their terms are reviewed.
P2: computer vision, OCR, POS integrations, mobile app. Not started, and not promised.

## Technical risks

- **Terms of use.** A permissive `robots.txt` is not permission. Retail connectors ship disabled.
- **Overpass availability.** The public instance could not be reached from the development
  machine during this phase; the connector is tested against recorded fixtures and the live
  call is unverified.
- **Tile servers.** The public OpenStreetMap tile server is not for heavy production use; the tile
  URL is configurable and the guide names alternatives.
- **Dependency weight.** Scrapy adds Twisted and lxml to the backend image; accepted in ADR 0010.
